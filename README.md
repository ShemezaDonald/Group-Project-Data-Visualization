# Adidas U.S. Sales Dashboard – Tableau Visualization

An interactive sales analytics dashboard built using Tableau to evaluate product performance, revenue trends, and customer segmentation for Adidas U.S. The dashboard enables business stakeholders to monitor KPIs, uncover high-performing categories, and make informed decisions around marketing and inventory strategies.

---

## 📌 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw retail sales data into actionable insights through visual analytics. The dashboard supports decision-makers by providing real-time visibility into revenue growth, sales distribution across product lines, location-based performance, and customer behavior patterns.

Designed with executive usability in mind, this work showcases how effective visualization can accelerate strategic conversations and operational planning.

---

## 📊 2. Data Description

The dataset includes:

- 🧾 Transactional sales data (SKU-level)
- 📍 Geographical segmentation (U.S. regional performance)
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer demographic attributes
- 🛒 Sales volume, profit margin, and discount rates
- 📈 Marketing and seasonal performance indicators

Preprocessing was conducted to ensure structure, consistency, and integration into Tableau.

---

## 🎨 3. Dashboard Features

✔ Revenue and profit tracking over time  
✔ Product category performance comparison  
✔ Customer segmentation and purchase behavior  
✔ Geographical sales distribution heatmaps  
✔ Top-seller breakdown and trend forecasting visuals  

The dashboard layout emphasizes accessibility for leadership teams and supports data-driven strategic planning.
